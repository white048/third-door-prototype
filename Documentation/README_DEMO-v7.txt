第三扇门 / THE THIRD DOOR
第七版 Windows 预览 · 拼贴长卷与交付校样

启动
双击 PlayDemo.cmd，以 1600×900 窗口游玩；也可直接运行 TheThirdDoor.exe。
标题页选择案例和语言。有进行中存档时，可选“继续上次”。
01 借来的声音：家人请你帮忙劝说，一个担心被疏远，一个想自己做决定。
02 事由的空白：同事想调整评审时间，也想保留私人原因。你要把工作请求带到。
每案三轮、六次回应，有两封话、七张一次性动作卡和七种结局。
两个案例都可以直接开始，结局记录分别保存。

操作
WASD 行走，鼠标观察，Shift 快走。E 倾听、休息或查看展台。
1 / 2 / 3 或点击选项回应。点击卡牌或按 Q 切换本次动作。
反馈会等你读完，按空格 / E 或点击继续。
还没回应时，Esc 返回展厅，不消耗回应；探索时，Esc 打开暂停。
F 在门前处理传话。Tab 打开手记、传话夹、逐句记录和结局档案。
G：获得重述卡后，可撤回最后一次回应。用过的卡不会退回。
00 长椅每轮可休息一次；03 出口随时可用。
R 重玩当前案例，F2 即时切换语言。暂停时，请点菜单里的“重新开始”。
结局后：空格收起 / 展开纸面；按住 Alt 点击菜单；N 切换案例；Home 返回目录。
右侧拼贴展台可按 E 放大阅读，结局后也能查看。阅读不消耗资源或回应。
V 展开本局拼贴长卷，也可在 Tab 手记中选择。展厅里的实体纸卷可按 E 打开。
滚轮或上下箭头阅读，左侧跳到各轮；F2 换语言时保留阅读位置。

怎么传话
每人保留一封话，里面有要你转告的原句，也可能有私下说的需要。
回到说话的人门前，可问私下内容能否转述，或退回传话承诺。
到另一人的门前，选择实际交付的内容；界面会显示资源和信任的变化。
点击交付会先打开校样，显示实际将说出的完整一句、许可和代价。
确认才会送出；“返回改选”或 Esc 关闭校样，不消耗资源，也不改变信任或同意。
信任高不代表允许公开私事。转述许可和愿不愿意直接谈，需要分别确认。
擅自转述后，可以回来承认并询问对方的意愿；对方未必愿意重新答应。
已经被别人听到的话，以及休息后的状态，不能用重述卡撤回。

资源
余力 0–8：听下去、问下去的精力。耗尽时，追问不会增加理解。
承重 0–8：还有多少事等你处理。达到 6 后，承接和追问多消耗一格余力。
直连 0–6：双方直接交流的准备程度。这个数值不能替任何一方答应谈话。
手记可查双方理解、信任、许可和每次回应。资源与卡牌的具体代价以选项预览为准。

存档
回应、休息、实际传话和重述后自动保存一个进行中案例。
返回目录后可继续。恢复时从展厅入口出发，轮次、卡牌、记录、许可和撤回机会保留。
开始新案例会替换进行中存档；结局会结束该存档，已发现的结局仍按案例保留。
存档在本地，有完整性校验和备份，没有云同步。

ENGLISH QUICK START
Run PlayDemo.cmd, choose a case, then ENTER IN ENGLISH. Either case is available from the start.
In the family case, two people ask you to help them get through to each other.
In the office case, a colleague wants a different review time without discussing private reasons.
Each case has three rounds, six responses, two envelopes, seven single-use cards and seven endings.

WASD and mouse: move and look. Shift: walk faster. E: listen, rest or inspect a print.
1–3 or click: respond. Q or click: select a card. SPACE / E: continue after feedback.
ESC before responding returns to the gallery without spending a response. In the gallery, ESC pauses.
F at a door handles messages. TAB opens notes, correspondence, the transcript and the ending archive.
Ask the speaker before sharing a private need. Ask separately whether they want to talk directly.
High trust does not grant permission. A public work request can be delivered without sharing private reasons.
G revises your last response once you have the final-round card. Spent cards stay spent.
Rest and message actions end the chance to revise that response. You cannot undo words already delivered.
Bench 00 offers one rest per round. Exit 03 lets you leave at any time.
R restarts in the gallery. Use the restart button while paused. F2 changes language without losing progress.
After an ending: SPACE folds the papers, ALT frees the cursor, N switches case, HOME opens the title.
E at the right-side print opens visual notes. Reading costs no resource, card or response.
V opens this run's collage strip. You can also open it from TAB or press E at the physical paper roll.
Scroll with the mouse wheel or UP / DOWN. The left buttons jump by round. F2 keeps your reading position.
Delivery opens a proof showing the complete words, permission and costs. Confirm to say them.
CHANGE SELECTION or ESC returns without spending resources or changing trust or consent.

BREATH (0–8) is the energy you have to listen. When it runs out, asking gives you no new insight.
LOAD (0–8) is the work left to you. At 6 or more, carrying and asking cost an extra BREATH.
BRIDGE (0–6) tracks readiness to talk directly. Both people must still agree for themselves.
Check the response preview for exact costs, and the notebook for trust, understanding and past choices.

Active progress saves locally after responses, rest, message actions and revision.
CONTINUE restores that progress at the gallery entrance. Starting another case replaces the active save.
An ending closes the active save; discoveries remain in that case's archive. There is no cloud sync.
